# Phase 9 — The brand, in context

Scope: Craftsmanship, Projects / Lookbook, About, Architects & Designers, Contact. Extend the selected dark cinematic direction to these routes; preserve existing catalogue and bespoke behavior. No Phase 10 AI work or new dependencies.

## Routes and purpose

- `/craftsmanship`: material-to-object narrative with seven stages, existing workshop/product imagery and clear distinctions between photographic detail and documented processes.
- `/projects`: filterable Residential / Hospitality / Commercial application studies. These are explicitly illustrative directions using supplied images, not completed projects or proof of suitability.
- `/projects/[slug]`: an individual study, its design considerations, collection reference and contextual project brief CTA.
- `/about`: stone, form and everyday use as the brand philosophy. No invented founder, history, capacity, certifications or people.
- `/trade`: preparation for custom sizing, sourcing, samples, technical drawings, quantities and delivery discussions; no service availability or shipping promises. A useful downloadable briefing checklist.
- `/contact`: clear paths to a personal brief, trade brief and material/sample discussion. No invented phone, email, social accounts or address. Reuse the Phase 8 inquiry rather than duplicate it.

## System

Reusable editorial shell, figure, closing invitation and contextual brief link. Typed local project-study repository supports future verified case studies. Native radio filters, semantic sections and disclosure controls; small client islands. Global navigation/footer expose all completed routes. Responsive menu used when eight destinations cannot fit comfortably.
