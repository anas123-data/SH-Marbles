SH MARBLES — Trade project briefing checklist

Use this checklist to prepare a conversation. It is not a product specification,
quotation, service commitment or submitted inquiry.

PROJECT
[ ] Project name / internal reference
[ ] Project setting: residential, hospitality, commercial or other
[ ] Primary contact, company and preferred contact method
[ ] Project country and delivery city / area
[ ] Desired timeline, key approvals and installation constraints

PIECES
[ ] Product types and quantities, grouped by room / zone
[ ] Approximate dimensions in millimetres
[ ] Intended use, loading, fixing and support requirements
[ ] Preferred shape, edge direction and finish
[ ] Natural variation and matching expectations

MATERIAL REVIEW
[ ] Preferred stone name, if verified, or colour direction
[ ] Actual sample / finish sample requested
[ ] Intended environment and cleaning / care requirements
[ ] Material identity, suitability and availability to confirm

DRAWINGS AND REFERENCES
[ ] Plans, elevations, sections and details needed
[ ] Required drawing file formats and units
[ ] Tap / plumbing positions for basins
[ ] Interfaces with joinery, worktops, floors or other trades
[ ] Reference images and drawing filenames

DELIVERY DISCUSSION
[ ] Packing and protection requirements
[ ] Site access, lifting / handling and storage constraints
[ ] Delivery responsibility and installation scope to confirm
[ ] Budget preference and currency, if known

Before proceeding, confirm feasibility, specifications, pricing, sample and
drawing availability, production schedule, shipping coverage and terms with
SH MARBLES. No availability, capacity, lead time or commercial terms are promised
by this checklist.
